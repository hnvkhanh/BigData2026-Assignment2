TIME / LEAKAGE REMINDER
For a historical snapshot at time t, filter rows by the documented row-time: first_seen_at for PullRequests, Issues, and PRReviews; created_at for IssueComments, PRReviewComments, and Pushes. Do not treat old created_at/submitted_at values as proof that a row was already available.
Fields such as merged_at, closed_at, and dismissed_at are known only later; use them only when their value is earlier than t.
